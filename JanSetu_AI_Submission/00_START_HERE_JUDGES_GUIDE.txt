💡 WHAT MAKES JANSETU AI UNIQUE (Executive Summary):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• The Anchor: Links directly to UP's existing Family ID (एक परिवार, एक पहचान).
• Proactive, Not Reactive: Citizen doesn't ask "what scheme do I get?".
  The assistant auto-evaluates the household and speaks in vernacular voice:
  "Rameshwar ji, your family is eligible for Rs. 52,000/yr across 4 schemes!"
• Handwriting & Scan OCR: Reads handwritten Tehsil income certificates and Khatauni.
• Ending in Something Filed: Submits to e-District DPI and returns legal receipt.
